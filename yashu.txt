A little ant fell into a clear stream while trying to drink water. A white dove sitting in a nearby tree saw the ant struggle. The dove quickly dropped a large leaf into the water near the ant.
The ant climbed onto the leaf and floated safely to the dry bank.
Days later, a hunter aimed his net at the dove in the tree. The ant saw this danger and bit the hunter hard on the foot. The hunter yelled in pain and dropped his net. The dove flew away safely, thankful to the tiny ant.
